# ClaimSorted product summary (public information, paraphrased)

ClaimSorted is a tech-enabled Third Party Administrator (TPA) for insurance.
Insurers hand it their whole claims operation instead of buying software.
It combines AI with human claims experts, and sells itself on a better
policyholder experience, faster cycles, and lower costs for the insurer.

## The claims journey, end to end

1. FNOL (First Notice of Loss). The policyholder reports a loss through a
   white-label electronic Notice of Loss, branded for the insurer, or through
   a claims portal where they can track status and upload documents.
2. Triage. The claim is sorted by severity and complexity, then assigned to
   a handler.
3. Coverage verification. The team checks the loss against the policy wording:
   is the policy active, is the incident covered, what exclusions apply, what
   deductible and limits apply.
4. Reserving. An estimate of what the claim will ultimately cost is set early
   and revised as facts emerge.
5. Investigation. Documents, photos, and third-party records are gathered
   and checked.
6. Decision. The claim is approved, denied with an explanation, negotiated,
   or subrogated (recovered from a responsible third party).
7. Payment and closure. Once adjudication passes and fraud checks are
   acceptable, payment is issued and the claim is closed.

## Fraud scoring

Proprietary algorithms flag unusual patterns, document tampering, and
excessive costs. The output is a fraud score with a pass-or-fail style
result. High-risk claims are routed to specialist investigators before
adjuster time and reserves are committed. The system also flags recovery
and subrogation opportunities.

## Human approval gates

AI automates document ingestion and decision workflows, but humans stay in
the loop for accuracy and empathy. Material decisions, especially payouts,
go through human oversight. This matters because the insurer remains
accountable to the regulator even when a TPA handles the claim, so every
decision needs an audit trail: who acted, what they decided, why, and when.

## Company claims (not independently audited)

ClaimSorted claims roughly three times faster claims cycles than a
traditional TPA, leakage below 1.2 percent on partner audits, and reopen
rates below 1.0 percent within 30 days. It serves more than 30 MGAs and
carriers, across property, auto, liability, accident and health, and
warranty lines of business.
