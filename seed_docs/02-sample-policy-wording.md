# Sample home insurance policy wording (FICTIONAL — for learning only)

Insurer: Fictional Mutual Insurance Ltd. Policy: HomeGuard Standard.
This document is invented for a demo. It is not a real policy.

## Section 1: What is covered

This policy covers sudden and accidental physical loss or damage to your
home and contents caused by: fire, lightning, explosion, storm, flood,
escape of water from fixed tanks and pipes, theft following forcible entry,
and accidental damage to fixed glass and sanitary fittings.

## Section 2: Exclusions — what is NOT covered

This policy does NOT cover:
- Damage caused gradually, including wear and tear, damp, and rot.
- Damage caused by faulty workmanship or defective materials.
- Theft where there is no sign of forcible entry.
- Loss of cash, jewellery, or valuables left unattended in a vehicle.
- Damage caused deliberately by you or anyone living with you.
- Claims arising from incidents that happened before the policy start date.

## Section 3: Deductibles and limits

The standard deductible (excess) is 250 currency units per claim, payable
by the policyholder. The storm deductible is 500. The single-item limit for
valuables is 2,000, and the total contents limit is 50,000. Claims above
the policy limit are paid only up to the limit.

## Section 4: How to claim

Notify us within 30 days of the incident. Provide photos of the damage,
proof of ownership for claimed items, and any police report reference for
theft claims. We may appoint an investigator for claims above 5,000.
Settlement is paid by bank transfer once the claim is approved, minus the
deductible. If we deny a claim, we will explain the reason in writing,
including which exclusion applies.
